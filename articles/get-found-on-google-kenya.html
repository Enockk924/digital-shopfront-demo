How to Get Your Business Found on Google in Kenya

When someone needs a service, a shop, a restaurant or a professional nearby, one of the first places they may look is Google. If your business information is missing, incomplete or out of date, a potential customer may choose another provider. The good news is that you can take useful first steps without paying for an advert.

1. Create or claim your Google Business Profile

Google Business Profile helps eligible businesses manage how they appear on Google Search and Maps. Visit the official Google Business Profile website and check whether a profile for your business already exists. If it does, follow Google's process to request access. If not, create a profile and provide accurate information. Google may ask you to verify that you represent the business.

2. Keep your business details consistent

Use the real business name customers know, the correct phone number, accurate opening hours and the right service area or address. If you serve customers at their locations rather than at a public shop, review Google's service-area business guidance. Do not create multiple profiles for the same business or use a false address.

3. Choose clear categories and describe your services

Select the category that most accurately describes your main business. Add the services you genuinely provide and write a short, specific description. Explain who you help, what you do and where you operate. Avoid stuffing the description with repeated keywords.

4. Add real photos and useful updates

Good, current photos can help people understand what to expect. Use genuine images of your premises, products, team or completed work, where appropriate and with permission. Keep updates factual. Never use another business's photos as if they were yours.

5. Ask customers for honest reviews

Invite real customers to share their experience. A polite follow-up message with your review link can make this easier. Do not buy reviews, offer rewards in exchange for positive reviews or write reviews for yourself. Respond professionally to feedback, including criticism.

6. Connect your website and measure what happens

Add your website address to your profile if you have one. Make sure the website shows the same contact details and services. Google Search Console can help website owners understand how their site appears in Google Search, identify indexing issues and review search performance.

A simple first-week checklist

Confirm that your profile is yours; correct your business name, phone and hours; add an accurate description and services; upload a few genuine photos; ask a small number of real customers for honest reviews; and check that your website and profile details match.
