Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Read our Terms of Use · Contact LOOK UP DIGITAL

LOOK UP DIGITAL, operating through AI Digital Business Studio (“LOOK UP DIGITAL”, “we”, “us” or “our”), respects your privacy. This notice explains what information may be handled when you visit our website, prepare an enquiry or use our AI website assistant.

Please do not submit sensitive or confidential information to the website assistant. This includes passwords, M-Pesa PINs, payment-card details, identity-document numbers or copies, health information and confidential business records. Contact us by email if your enquiry requires private discussion.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

LOOK UP DIGITAL, operating through AI Digital Business Studio, is responsible for the personal information handled for the purposes described in this notice. Our privacy contact is lookupdigital.business@gmail.com.

2. Information we may handle

Depending on how you use the website, the information may include:

  • Enquiry details: your name, email address, selected service and the project information you choose to include in an email or WhatsApp enquiry.
  • AI assistant messages: the text you choose to submit to the website assistant and the response generated for you.
  • Technical information: information that may be processed by website hosting, network, security and service providers when your browser requests website content. This can include an IP address, browser or device information, request time and diagnostic information, depending on the provider and its configuration.
  • External service interactions: information handled by a third-party service when you choose to follow a link or use that service.

Please provide only information that is relevant to your enquiry.

3. Contact form and WhatsApp enquiries

The website contact form does not submit your details directly to a LOOK UP DIGITAL database. When you choose “Prepare enquiry by email”, the information you entered is used to create an email draft on your device. It is sent to us only if you review and send that email through your email service. Your email provider may process the draft and message under its own terms and privacy notice.

When you choose the WhatsApp option, the website prepares a message and opens WhatsApp. The message is sent to us only when you choose to send it in WhatsApp. WhatsApp and its provider, Meta, process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

We use enquiries to respond to you, understand the project you are considering, prepare a quotation where requested and manage any resulting business relationship.

4. AI website assistant

The website assistant is an automated service, not a human operator. When you submit a message, the website sends its text to our assistant endpoint hosted on Cloudflare Workers. The Worker is configured with a Cloudflare Workers AI binding, which is used to generate an automated response. We have not identified or verified the specific model name, so we do not make a model-specific claim in this notice.

Cloudflare's published Workers AI data-usage information says that, for standard inference requests that do not use a stateful feature, prompts and generated outputs are processed to provide the service and are not retained after they are no longer needed for that processing. Cloudflare also states that Workers AI customer content is not used to train its AI models. Cloudflare explains that separate storage, caching, application logging or other stateful features can create different data-handling arrangements. See Cloudflare Workers AI: Data usage.

Our Worker has operational logging enabled. We have not completed a technical audit to establish whether any application-level logs include message content or the exact retention period for those logs. For that reason, do not use the assistant to send sensitive, confidential or high-risk information. We will update this notice if our technical review establishes more specific details or if the assistant's configuration changes.

Assistant messages are used to generate a response to the question you submit and to operate and protect the assistant service. Do not rely on an AI response as a quotation, contract, professional advice or confirmation that a service is available; contact us directly for confirmation.

5. Why we use information

We use information only where it is relevant to operating the website and responding to you. This may include:

  • responding to questions, service enquiries and partnership proposals;
  • understanding the service or project you have asked about;
  • operating, maintaining, troubleshooting and protecting the website and assistant;
  • keeping appropriate business records and meeting applicable legal obligations; and
  • addressing misuse, security incidents or disputes where necessary.

Where required by law, we will ask for consent before carrying out a particular activity. We do not use the website enquiry form as a mailing-list subscription, and we do not currently operate a marketing newsletter through this website.

6. Service providers and third-party websites

The website relies on third-party services to operate and display content. These currently include:

  • GitHub Pages: website hosting and delivery;
  • Cloudflare: hosting the assistant endpoint and providing the Workers AI service;
  • Google Fonts: delivery of font files used by the website;
  • Unsplash: delivery of some illustrative service photographs; and
  • your chosen email or WhatsApp provider: when you choose to prepare or send an enquiry using those services.

When your browser loads content from an external provider, that provider may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser request information. When you follow a link to another website or tool, that service operates independently and its own privacy notice and terms apply. A link does not mean that LOOK UP DIGITAL owns, controls or endorses that service.

7. International processing

Some of the providers supporting this website operate services or infrastructure in more than one country. As a result, technical information and, where applicable, assistant messages may be processed outside Kenya. The exact location can depend on the provider, service and configuration. We will take applicable Kenyan data-protection requirements and relevant safeguards into account when using providers that process information internationally. Please consult the providers' own privacy and data-processing information for further details about their services.

8. How long information is kept

We aim to keep personal information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose for which it was collected, or for a period required to meet legal, accounting or dispute-resolution obligations. Enquiry emails may remain in the relevant email accounts for as long as needed to respond, manage a potential or actual project and maintain appropriate business records.

For AI assistant messages, Cloudflare's standard Workers AI inference retention commitment is described in section 4. Any separate application logging, storage or other stateful processing is subject to the relevant configuration and provider arrangements. We have not yet verified a precise retention period for any such application-level records. We will not state a fixed period until it has been checked.

9. Cookies, analytics and advertising

The current website code does not intentionally install advertising or analytics tracking scripts. The website loads fonts and images from external providers, which may receive technical request information when those resources are requested. Your browser may also store ordinary technical data as part of normal website operation.

Advertising cookies, behavioural advertising and online payment processing are not currently active on this website. If we introduce analytics, advertising technologies, mailing-list tools or payment services, we will review this notice and provide any notices or consent choices required by applicable law before activating them.

10. Recommended tools and affiliate links

The recommended digital tools currently shown on the website are direct links to provider websites, not paid endorsements or affiliate links. We do not currently claim a partnership with those providers. If we introduce an affiliate relationship or sponsored placement in future, we will identify it clearly.

11. How we protect information

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the website and the information handled to reduce the risk of unauthorised access, misuse, loss or disclosure. Website connections use HTTPS. We also aim to limit the information requested to what is relevant to an enquiry. No internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so please avoid sending sensitive information through the website assistant.
