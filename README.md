# Look Up Digital

**Helping Businesses Get Found Online.**

A responsive starter website for Look Up Digital, a digital services studio being developed for businesses in Kenya and East Africa.

## Included in this starter

- Responsive homepage and mobile navigation
- Services overview for websites, online shops, social media/content, branding, AI solutions and website support
- About, Contact and preliminary Privacy pages
- Insights/blog section placeholder
- Reserved future integration points for social profiles, WhatsApp Business, a dedicated business email and Google AdSense

## Project structure

- `index.html` — homepage
- `about.html` — about page
- `contact.html` — contact information placeholder
- `privacy.html` — preliminary privacy notice
- `assets/css/styles.css` — responsive visual design
- `assets/js/main.js` — mobile navigation and footer year

## Before public launch

1. Confirm the final registered business identity and suitable domain.
2. Set up a dedicated business email and WhatsApp Business contact.
3. Create/confirm official social media profiles and add their real URLs.
4. Replace contact placeholders with verified business details.
5. Publish original articles and complete the privacy/cookie information before enabling analytics or advertising.
6. Add Google AdSense only after the site has substantial original content and meets Google's current programme policies. Approval is not guaranteed.

No personal email, invented contact details, live advertising scripts, tracking scripts, payment processing or non-functional enquiry form are included.

## Preview

Open `index.html` in a browser or deploy this static site to a compatible static host. This branch is a development version; the repository's `main` branch has not been changed.
